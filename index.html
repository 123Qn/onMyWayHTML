Features

Built for people who love to wander.

Every trip on onMyWay is a set of ordered stops on a real map, so the places you loved become a route anyone can take.

Trips, stop by stop

Add up to 20 places in order, with notes and photos for each stop. Your trip becomes a story on the map.

See the route

Every trip shows markers and a route line by car, on foot or by bike, so you can see exactly how someone travelled.

Like, comment, follow

React to trips in your feed, then tap “Follow this trip” to open every stop in Google Maps or Apple Maps.

How it works

Three steps. Endless trips.

From your last weekend away to a month-long adventure, sharing takes minutes.

  1. Create a trip

    Pick your stops in the order you visited them.

  2. Add the details

    Notes, tips and photos for every place along the way.

  3. Share and inspire

    Friends explore your route and follow it on their own trip.
