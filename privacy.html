The short version

  • We collect what you need to use onMyWay: your account details, the trips you create, and the photos and places you add to them.
  • We only use your device location while you are using the app, and only if you allow it.
  • We may show sponsored places (for example a restaurant or attraction) that are clearly labelled. Businesses that advertise never receive your name, email or exact location, and we do not sell your personal information.
  • You can edit or delete your trips at any time, and you can delete your whole account.

1. Who we are

onMyWay (“onMyWay”, “we”, “us”) is a social app for travellers. People create trips made of ordered stops, with notes and photos, and share them so others can view the route on a map and follow it.

This policy explains how we handle personal information when you use the onMyWay mobile app and the website at getonmyway.app (together, the “Service”). onMyWay is operated by [YOUR NAME OR BUSINESS NAME], based in [CITY], Australia. We handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and, where they apply to you, other privacy laws such as the GDPR.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account information: your email address and password when you sign up. Passwords are stored in hashed form by our authentication provider; we never see them in plain text. If you sign in with a third-party account (for example Google or Apple), we receive your name and email from that provider.
  • Profile information: your display name, username, profile photo and short bio, if you choose to add them.
  • Trips and content: trip titles, the places (stops) you add and their order, notes, and photos you upload. Photos may contain metadata such as the date and location they were taken.
  • Messages to us: anything you send when you contact us for support or feedback.

Information collected when you use the Service

  • Location: if you give permission, the app uses your device’s location while it is open to show where you are on the map and help you add stops. We do not track your location in the background. You can turn this off at any time in your device settings and still add stops by searching.
  • Usage and device information: basic technical data such as device type, operating system, app version, crash reports and the date and time of requests. We use this to keep the Service working and fix problems.
  • Website: our website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs (such as IP address and browser type) for security.

3. How we use your information

  • To create and manage your account and let you sign in.
  • To store, display and share the trips you create, including showing stops and route lines on a map.
  • To show your trips in other users’ feeds, according to your settings.
  • To keep the Service secure, prevent abuse and enforce our Terms of Use.
  • To fix bugs, understand which features are used and improve the Service.
  • To show sponsored places that are relevant to the area you are viewing or travelling in, and to give advertisers combined statistics about how their listings perform (see section 5).
  • To reply to you and send important messages about your account or changes to the Service. We will only send marketing emails if you opt in, and you can unsubscribe at any time.

4. What other people can see

onMyWay is a social network, so some information is visible to others:

  • Your display name, username, profile photo and bio.
  • Trips you publish, including their stops, notes, photos and map route.

Think carefully before adding a stop that reveals a private place, such as your home address. When someone taps “Follow this trip”, the stop locations are opened in Google Maps or Apple Maps on their device; those apps are run by Google and Apple under their own privacy policies.

5. Sponsored places and ads

To keep onMyWay free, local businesses such as restaurants, cafés, hotels and attractions may pay to have their place shown more prominently, for example on the map, in search results or as a suggested stop. These are always marked as “Sponsored”.

How we choose what to show you

  • We use the general area you are viewing on the map or travelling in, and the type of trip you are looking at (for example a food trip or a hiking trip).
  • We do not build advertising profiles from your private information, and we do not use your photos or notes to target ads.

What advertisers receive

  • Only combined, non-identifying statistics, such as how many people saw or tapped their listing, or how many added it to a trip.
  • Advertisers never receive your name, email, profile, exact location or the trips you have created.

Third-party ad networks

We do not currently use third-party ad networks or track you across other companies’ apps and websites. If we ever do, we will update this policy first and, where required (for example under Apple’s App Tracking Transparency), ask for your permission before any tracking takes place.

7.Where your information is stored

8.How we protect your information

  • All data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS).
  • Access to data is restricted by database rules so that users can only change their own content.
  • Passwords are hashed by our authentication provider and never stored in plain text.

No system is completely secure. If we become aware of a data breach that is likely to cause you serious harm, we will notify you and the relevant authorities as required by law.

9. How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is active. If you delete a trip or photo, it is removed from the Service straight away and from our backups within [30] days. If you delete your account, see section 11.

10.Your choices and rights

  • Access and correction: you can view and edit your profile and trips in the app at any time, or ask us for a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Location: you can turn location access on or off in your device settings.
  • Marketing: you can unsubscribe from any marketing email using the link in it.
  • Complaints: if you have a concern, please contact us first and we will respond within 30 days. If you are not satisfied, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au, or to your local data protection authority.

11.Deleting your account

You can delete your account in the app under Profile → Settings → Delete account. If you can’t access the app, email us at privacy@getonmyway.app from the email address on your account and ask us to delete it.

When you delete your account, we delete your profile, trips, stops, notes and photos within [30] days. We may keep a small amount of information for longer where the law requires it or to prevent fraud and abuse.

12.Children

onMyWay is not intended for children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect information from children under that age. If you believe a child has given us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

13.Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as onMyWay grows. When we make important changes, we will update the date at the top and let you know in the app or by email before the changes take effect.
